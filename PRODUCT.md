# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Owners of nucula v2 boards setting up or maintaining their device with a computer
and a USB-C data cable, without an embedded development environment. The homepage
also explains the open hardware and firmware to prospective builders.

## Product Purpose

Make browser-based installation, updates and basic configuration the main way to
set up nucula. Keep the firmware's serial console available as an advanced tool.

## Brand Personality

Direct, technical, restrained. Preserve the existing monochrome identity shared
with the btc++ talk deck, documented in README.md.

## Anti-references

Do not replace the existing identity with a new visual theme. Do not make a raw
console or development-environment instructions the primary setup experience.

## Design Principles

- Present two entry points: connect to installed nucula firmware for Wi-Fi and the
  console, or install/update firmware directly over USB, including blank boards.
- Show device replies and verification results before claiming success.
- Protect wallet storage and make failed operations recoverable.
- Explain browser and cable requirements where users need them.
- Reveal advanced console controls on demand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use labeled native forms, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus and readable
contrast. Communicate connection state in text as well as color. Respect reduced
motion and support narrow viewports, while explaining desktop browser requirements.
